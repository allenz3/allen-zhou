Direct Transfer

Dynamics 365 Conversation Control · Primary frontend owner

Direct Transfer lets a service representative transfer an active conversation to another representative without first starting a consultation. I owned the frontend for approximately six months, from implementation through global production rollout.

I built it in React, TypeScript, and Redux, integrating REST APIs and new state handling. I also refactored shared transfer logic so warm and cold transfers could reuse the same architecture, including error recovery and configurable voice, messaging, and timeout behavior.

  • Added 100+ unit tests, with integration, critical E2E, accessibility, and manual validation.
  • Released through private preview, pre-production validation, and staged regional deployment using feature flags and telemetry.
  • Production telemetry showed approximately 5–10 seconds lower average resolution time associated with the workflow.
  • No known customer incidents were attributable specifically to Direct Transfer after the completed launch.

Microsoft Learn: Enable direct transfer of conversations
Public general availability: July 31, 2025.

Consult to Queue

Dynamics 365 Conversation Control · Primary frontend owner

This feature lets representatives consult a queue so the service can find an eligible expert, instead of requiring them to know who to contact. I led substantial frontend development and ownership across approximately a year of planning, implementation, testing, and rollout.

I generalized the existing consult architecture to share logic between individual and queue consultation, integrated the queue-routing endpoint, and handled asynchronous participant events and conversation-state changes.

  • Added or maintained hundreds of unit tests around the consult architecture and supported approximately a dozen integration scenarios.
  • Validated accessibility, localization, right-to-left layouts, and responsive behavior during staged global rollout.
  • Improved response performance by approximately 2–3 seconds.

Direct Transfer and Consult to Queue together represented approximately 60–70% of weekly activity within the relevant Conversation Control workflows.

Microsoft Learn: Select a representative automatically in consult to queue
Enhanced capability general availability: January 31, 2026.

Browser-based regression testing

Intent Assist · Playwright MCP, Azure DevOps

Built AI-assisted browser automation for approximately 30–40 end-to-end scenarios across first-party workflows, third-party integrations, voice, CRM, customer-facing flows, and failure states.

The tooling used persistent browser profiles and pre-warmed authentication for multi-user testing. I standardized configuration and result schemas, added automated reporting, and integrated validation into Azure DevOps CI/CD.

Recurring regression-validation time fell from approximately two hours to under 30 minutes, a reduction of more than 75%.

During my final several months, AI-assisted development also increased my average PR submission cadence from roughly two per week to two per day. Code review, testing, accessibility checks, and release validation remained part of the workflow.
